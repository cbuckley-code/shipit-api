# Writing a ShipIt ADR

1. Copy the shape of `docs/adr-001-pricing-tiers.md`: **Status / Context / Decision / Consequences**.
2. Number it next in sequence: `docs/adr-NNN-short-slug.md`.
3. Keep it under a page — decisions, not essays.
4. Cite the product brief or an existing ADR wherever the context comes from one.
5. Status starts as `Proposed`; a human flips it to `Accepted` after review.
